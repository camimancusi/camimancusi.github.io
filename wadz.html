WADZ+

WADZ+ is an investment platform where users deposit funds into curated yield strategies through a web app, with a referral program on top. It runs in production with real funds, so delivery speed and quality have to go together.

The challenge

Three workstreams in parallel

The wallet app, additional vaults and the referral program moved at the same time, each with its own scope and acceptance.

Real money in production

Testing had to cover the live app with real funds, not only staging.

What I did

  1. 01
    Delivery

    I led the client kickoff and run biweekly invoicing and milestone acceptance across the three workstreams. I’m the client’s day-to-day point of contact.

  2. 02
    Quality

    I test the production app and the admin backoffice, and I keep QA scope explicit: client deliverables in, internal tooling out.

  3. 03
    Communication

    Clear status, risks and decisions to the client, so milestone acceptance never comes as a surprise.

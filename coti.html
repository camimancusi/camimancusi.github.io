COTI Snap

COTI Snap is a MetaMask plugin and companion web app that let users manage private, encrypted assets right inside their wallet.

Role
Project Manager
Team
3 · two full-stack developers and a PO
Platform
MetaMask Snap + companion web app
Link
metamask.coti.io ↗

The challenge

An external timeline

Publishing depended on MetaMask’s review, a timeline we didn’t control.

A small team, two products

The same two developers covered both the plugin and the web app.

What I did

  1. 01
    Scope

    I defined scope with the client so the small team could focus on what mattered for launch.

  2. 02
    QA coordination

    I coordinated testing of the onboarding flow, the most sensitive part of the experience.

  3. 03
    Release

    I managed the release and the publication process.

The result

Shipped and published in the official MetaMask Snaps directory.
